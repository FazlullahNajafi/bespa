##### &#x09;**The manual way of booking a taxi is:**

##### &#x09;Step 1: Call to a well-known driver OR go to the taxi/bus stand to book physically

##### &#x09;Step 2: Book a seat (usually not a desired one) either in a group fixed-journey (cheaper) OR personal or group (more expensive) journey

##### &#x09;Step 3: The driver fix the date and time and the passenger has to plan accordingly

##### &#x09;Step 4: This way, the driver gets 5-6 passengers(sometime not full)

##### &#x09;Step 5: The driver go to pick all passengers from their locations on the date and time

##### &#x09;Step 6: They start their journey 

##### &#x09;Step 7: They finish there journey and make their payments to the driver



##### &#x09;**The digitalised way using the app:**

##### &#x09;Step 1: The passenger browse all the desired drivers in the app and choose according to the user's time and date

##### &#x09;Step 3:	The user can also call the driver for confirmation about timing and seats

##### &#x09;Step 4: The user got confirmation msg after booking (optional)

##### &#x09;Step 5:	The driver will pick the passenger form their location; AND the user can get to know where the driver has reach to pick them through map (optional)

##### &#x09;**The manual way of booking a taxi is:**

##### &#x09;Step 1: Go to a place where taxi are available in large number(van);OR Go to the Eeco 	stand physically to book a seat for personal group;OR Go to bus stand and pick a bus and wait for sometime to fill the bus;OR Stand near a road and take a lift in these taxi

##### &#x09;Step 2: Book a seat (usually not a desired one) either in a group (cheaper) fixed journey OR personal or group (more expensive) journey

##### &#x09;Step 3: The driver wait until the passengers are full and they have to wait sometime 10-20 minutes

##### &#x09;Step 4: They start their journey

##### &#x09;Step 5: They finish their journey by reaching their personal destinations in the way of the fixed journey 

##### &#x09;Step 6: They make their payments to the driver either in cash or online

##### &#x09;Step 7: Sometime the passenger is not fully reached to its destination as the journey is fixed



##### &#x09;**The digitalised way using the app:**

##### &#x09;Step 1: The main goal is to find a desired taxi and seat at the moment

##### &#x09;Step 2: The user browse all the list of driver and choose according to his/her plan

##### &#x09;Step 3: To book a taxi for a personal group journey, the user will choose a driver and can call to confirm the date and time \[more expensive]

##### &#x09;Step 4: To book a usual seat for a fixed grouped journey, the user can see which driver is nearer to him/her, or can see how many seats are left in a taxi at the stand and the user will plan accordingly 

##### &#x09;Step 5: The passenger will choose a taxi and start the journey

##### &#x09;Step 6: They finish their journey and can rate the driver, and make their payment through the uploaded payment QR code
